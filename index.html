WHO WE ARE

A ministry for the Current and Next Generation.

JESUS FIRE IGNITERS (JEFIRE) is an interdenominational Christian group centered on transforming, molding and empowering youth; spiritually, socially and economically.

We are committed to reaching young people with the Gospel of Jesus Christ, discipling them in the Christian faith, and equipping them to become faithful servants and witnesses of Christ.

Gospel-Centered

Proclaiming Jesus Christ and the message of salvation.

Interdenominational

Welcoming young people across Christian denominations.

Evangelism

Taking the Gospel into communities through outreach.

Discipleship

Helping young people grow in God's Word and prayer.

REHABILITATION & EMPOWERMENT

Building a Youth Centre for Transformed Lives

We have a God-given dream of building a Youth Centre where youth can be equipped holistically.

Many young people who receive Christ desire to leave their former manner of life - stopping crime, leaving drugs, prostitution and other destructive lifestyles. They need a place to be settled, grounded in the Word of God, and equipped with life skills to earn a living and live a dignified new life in Christ.

✓ Biblical discipleship and counseling
✓ Life skills & vocational training
✓ Rehabilitation from drugs & crime
✓ Entrepreneurship & economic empowerment
✓ Safe housing & mentorship
