WHO WE ARE

A ministry for the Current and Next Generation.

JESUS FIRE IGNITERS (JEFIRE) is an interdenominational Christian group centered on transforming, molding and empowering youth; spiritually, socially and economically.

We are committed to reaching young people with the Gospel of Jesus Christ, discipling them in the Christian faith, and equipping them to become faithful servants and witnesses of Christ.

Gospel-Centered

Proclaiming Jesus Christ and the message of salvation.

Interdenominational

Welcoming young people across Christian denominations.

Evangelism

Taking the Gospel into communities through outreach.

Discipleship

Helping young people grow in God's Word and prayer.

OUR FOUR PILLARS

Know. Grow. Serve. Lead.

01

Evangelism

Proclaiming the Gospel and giving people an opportunity to hear and respond to Jesus Christ.

Matthew 28:19-20 " Therefore go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, and teaching them to obey everything I have commanded you. And surely I am with you always, to the very end of the age.”

02

Discipleship

Helping those who come to Christ grow in faith, understand God's Word and develop a consistent prayer life.

03

Youth Equipping

Building biblical knowledge, spiritual maturity, Christian values and a heart for serving God.

1 Timothy 4:12: "Let no one despise you for your youth, but set the believers an example in speech, in conduct, in love, in faith, in purity."

04

Leadership & Christian Service

Encouraging young people to discover their God-given gifts and serve Christ, the Church and their communities.

1 Peter 4:10: "As each has received a gift, use it to serve one another, as good stewards of God's varied grace."
